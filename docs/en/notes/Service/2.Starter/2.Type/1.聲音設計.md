## **Overview**  

::: important Introduction
**Sound Design** is the process of creating custom sound performances for animations, games, and films, including `environmental sounds`, `event sounds`, `UI sounds`, `ability sounds`, and `synthesized sound effects`.
:::

We provide the following types of sound design services:

### **Environmental Audio**
---
Used to build the foundational auditory space of a scene, including natural environments (wind, rain, ecological sounds) and man-made environments (mechanical operations, urban background noise).

::: card title="✅ Suitable Types"
 - Sounds with randomness
 - Loopable sounds
 - Sounds without a clear emission source
:::

::: card title="⚠️ Unsuitable Types"
 - Music or rhythmic elements
 - Interactive sounds
 - Implicit beats in the environment (e.g., clock ticking, mechanical rhythmic sounds)
 - Instant interaction sounds in the scene (e.g., door opening, item dropping)
:::
::::

## **Confirm Commission Requirements**

Once the project name is confirmed, we can proceed to commission the requirements. Here, we use sound design as an example:

<ImageCard
  image="/Images/services/Quote-SFX.png"
  title="Select the type of sound you need"
  description="Sound effects are divided into six categories. For detailed descriptions, refer to the guide."
/>

::: steps

1. **Select Sound Type**  <Badge type="tip" text="Type" />
   
    Confirm which sound type [+sfxtype] you need.
    [+sfxtype]: Sound types are currently divided into ==Environmental Audio==, ==Object Interaction SFX==, ==Event SFX==, ==UI Audio SFX==, ==Ability SFX==, and ==Kinematic Foley==.

2. **Choose Whether to Pair with Animation** [+withAnimation].

    [+withAnimation]: If your sound can be completed without visuals, pairing with animation is unnecessary. For example: AK47 gunfire, the sound of cutlery clashing, or a simple notification sound.
    If animation pairing is required, please provide reference videos during production.

3. **Fill in the Required Quantity**

    Specify the number of sounds needed. EX: If you need multiple variations of footsteps, fill in 2~4.

4. **Add Multiple Types of Requirements**

    If you need multiple types of sounds commissioned simultaneously, click the "+" button below to add another sound selection block.
    <ImageCard image="/Images/services/Quote-SFX-Add.png"/>

5. **Price Estimation**

    If all the above steps are completed correctly, you can get a price estimate for the sound effects of this type.
