## **Overview**  
::: important Introduction
**Music Production** is the creation of original music suitable for game backgrounds, videos, and animation soundtracks, including `ambient music`, `short music`, and `composition & arrangement`. We can provide specific scene music or theme song design according to your needs.
:::

We provide the following types of music production services:

### **Ambient Music**
---
Melody-free musical layers used to build the emotional foundation of scenes, emphasizing spatial feel and atmosphere creation.

::: card title="✅ Suitable Types"
 - Ambient soundscapes without obvious rhythm (Drone/Pad)
 - Dynamic layered loop segments (changes with game state)
 - Subtle atmosphere that complements the scene
:::

::: card title="⚠️ Unsuitable Types"
 - Theme music with strong melodies
 - Rhythmic dance/battle music
 - Tracks with vocal performances
 - Music segments shorter than 30 seconds
 - Non-loopable tracks
:::

### **Remix**
---

Professional recreation services based on existing musical materials.

:::: card-grid
::: card title="✅ Suitable Types"
- Stylized adaptations of theme music (e.g., 8-bit versions)
- Multi-track separated versions for gameplay streaming
- Battle music variants corresponding to dynamic difficulty
- Cross-cultural instrumental reinterpretations (e.g., Japanese shamisen adaptations)
:::

::: card title="⚠️ Unsuitable Types"
- Direct editing without original authorization
- Simple EQ/volume adjustments only
- Extreme adaptations that destroy original recognition
- Automatically generated AI music alternatives
:::
::::

---

- Dynamic layered versions of game music (adjusting instrument combinations based on game state)
- Orchestration resets optimized for specific platforms (e.g., reducing polyphony for mobile versions)
- Cultural localization adaptations (incorporating traditional instrument elements)
:::

::: card title="⚠️ Unsuitable Types"
- Completely changing the original harmony progression and melody lines
- Not preserving at least 50% of the original's recognizable features
- Simple tempo/key adjustments only
- Automatically generated AI adaptations
:::

## **Confirming Commission Requirements**

![alt text](/Images/services/Quote-Music.png)

::: steps

1. **Select Music Type**  <Badge type="tip" text="Type" />

    You need to confirm which category your **music type**[+Musictype] belongs to.
    [+Musictype]:Sound types are currently divided into ==Ambient Music==, ==Theme Music==, ==Cinematic Score==, ==Stingers & Bridges==, ==Remix==, and ==Re-arrangement==.

2. **Choose Whether to Include Animation**
   
    Video-type music usually needs to be synchronized with a timeline, while game music is matched to scene requirements, depending on the situation.


3. **Fill in Music Length**

    Fill in the required length of the musical piece, measured in seconds.

4. **Add Multiple Type Requirements**

    If you have multiple types of music that need to be commissioned simultaneously, please click the "+" button below to add a new music selection block.
    ![alt text](/Images/services/Quote-Music-Multiple.png)

5. **Price Calculation**

    If all the above steps are completed correctly, you can get a price estimate for your music type.
