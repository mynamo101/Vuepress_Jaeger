## **Personal Quotation**

After completing the order, the website will automatically redirect to the quotation page, where you can check if the information you just entered is correct.

![alt text](/Images/order/Your-Quotation.png)

---
You can also access it from this section on the homepage:

![alt text](/Images/order/Invoice-Block.png)

After entering, you can find the content you just filled out in the "Your Quotation" section.

![alt text](/Images/order/Your-Quotation-List.png)

### **Unsigned**

At this stage, the quotation is still unsigned. You need to confirm the content as soon as possible and provide an electronic signature or stamp.  
Click "Sign Now" in the list, and a window will pop up. After updating the signature, click "Update" to complete.

![alt text](/Images/order/Update-Sign.png)

### **Signed**

If your quotation has been signed, the list will display "**Signed**," and you can download it in PDF format for your records.

![alt text](/Images/order/Download-Quotation.png)

## **Edit/Delete Orders**

If you no longer want this commission, regardless of whether it has been signed, you can click "Edit/Delete" in the **Edit Field** to modify or delete it.
